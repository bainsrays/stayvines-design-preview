What it handles, and what it hands over.

Five jobs that eat an operator's evening. The AI takes the first four, and the fifth is the rule that keeps your team in charge of all of them.

Guest chat that runs through the night

Replies in seconds at 1am, on Sunday, during Sallah. It holds a natural back and forth in English and Nigerian Pidgin, remembers what the guest already asked, and never copy-pastes the same stiff paragraph twice.

Instant booking capture

Dates confirmed, unit held, payment link out, and the details written into your calendar while the guest is still in the chat.

Check-in support Gate code, directions, wifi, and the inverter switch, sent before anyone has to ask twice.

Human handoff with the full thread attached

When a guest is upset, a refund is on the table, or the request is genuinely unusual, Stayvines stops talking and tags a named teammate. They open the chat already knowing everything that was said, so nobody has to ask the guest to repeat themselves.

Every unit, one inbox

Whether you run three flats or twenty-eight units across Nigeria, each unit keeps its own rates, rules and lockbox notes. The AI never quotes the wrong apartment.
